06Fidelity modes & copyright

TubeBook lets you pick how closely the generated ebook follows the source video. Each mode carries different intellectual-property risk, and that risk is yours to manage.

6.1 — High Fidelity (close adaptation)

Produces a derivative work that closely tracks the source. You must own the video, have an explicit license from the rights holder, or be confident the source is in the public domain.

6.2 — Smart Adapt (default)

Rewrites and restructures the source into new long-form text. The output is generally safer for original publication but is still derived from the source idea; you remain responsible for verifying it does not reproduce protected expression.

6.3 — Low Fidelity (inspired)

Uses the video only as a thematic seed and produces substantially original content. Recommended for KDP, lead magnets, and commercial distribution where you do not own the source.

6.4 — Limited AI copyright

In many jurisdictions (including the US), purely AI-generated text is not copyrightable. Your editorial choices, custom instructions, and post-generation edits are what create human authorship. We make no warranty that any specific output is registrable or enforceable as your copyrighted work.
