Getting started

  1. Create a free account at the TubeBook app — no credit card required.
  2. Receive 50 free credits on signup (one-time, never refreshed) — enough for one lead magnet ebook.
  3. Open Generate, paste a YouTube URL or your own transcript, choose a mode, and submit.
  4. TubeBook handles everything — transcription, structuring, AI writing, optional cover and chapter images — then delivers exports in PDF, DOCX, EPUB, and PPTX.
⚡

Generating an ebook

Choose your ebook type

  • Lead magnet — short, punchy, ~5 chapters. Great for newsletter freebies and opt-ins. Costs 30 base credits.
  • Commercial ebook — long-form, ~10 chapters, deeper structure and styling. Ready to sell. Costs 80 base credits.

Input source

  • YouTube URL — TubeBook ingests and transcribes the video automatically. Adds +20 credits.
  • Paste a transcript — use any text transcript you already have. No extra charge for the source.

Images

Optional cover and per-chapter images. You choose how many images per chapter (0–3). Each chapter image costs +2 credits.

Troubleshooting

Generation seems stuck or is taking a long time
Generations resume automatically if a step is interrupted — no action needed. Most ebooks complete within a few minutes. Long commercial ebooks with multiple chapter images can take 5–10 minutes. If nothing has changed after 15 minutes, contact support.
YouTube URL won't ingest
Make sure the video is public and has captions (auto-generated or manual). Private, age-restricted, members-only, and live videos cannot be processed. Try pasting the transcript directly instead.
I can't find my ebook
Open Dashboard in the app. Ebooks are stored for 30 days after generation, then archived. Download anything you want to keep before then.
My credit balance seems wrong
Each generation deducts credits when it completes, not when it starts. If a generation fails, credits are refunded. Check the Activity tab in the app for a detailed credit log.
