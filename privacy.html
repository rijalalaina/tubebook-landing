By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

01Information we collect

Account data

  • Email address, display name, password hash (or OAuth identifier).
  • Billing data — handled by Stripe; we never store full card numbers.

Content you provide

  • Source material you submit for processing: YouTube URLs, web page URLs, pasted text, and uploaded Word, PowerPoint or PDF documents.
  • Text retrieved from web page URLs you supply. When you add a web page as a source, we fetch that page on your behalf and store its extracted text with your ebook so it can be used to write your chapters.
  • Generated ebooks, covers, chapter images, audiobook files, translations, edits, quality-check findings, and related publishing metadata.
  • Artwork you upload and the placement choices you make for images inside a book.
  • Writing samples you provide to build a personal style profile. The uploaded file is deleted after it is read; the resulting style description and short excerpts are kept with your profile so you can review, refine or delete them.
  • Topic research queries. The niche or topic you type into Find a topic, together with the results returned for it, are stored against your account so you can reopen the research without paying for it again. Research results are cached and may be reused to answer an equivalent query from another user — the cached record contains only the topic and public market data, never your identity or any of your source material.

Technical data

  • IP address, browser and device info, log timestamps.
  • Usage events (generations started, credits consumed, errors).

02How we use information

  • To operate the Service: process generations, deliver exports, track credit balance.
  • To bill subscriptions and credit packs via Stripe.
  • To send transactional emails (account, billing, security).
  • To send occasional product news and offers to account holders via our email provider (Brevo). Every such email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, honored immediately and permanently.
  • To debug, secure the platform, and improve quality.

03AI processing & sub-processors

To generate, edit, check or translate ebooks, the relevant content of your sources and book — video transcripts, extracted web or document text, pasted text, chapter text and instructions — is sent to the configured AI model providers through our server-side integrations. These providers process the content to return the requested result and do not use API content to train their models under the applicable business/API terms.

  • Supabase / Lovable Cloud — database, authentication, storage.
  • Stripe — payments and subscription management.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, edge runtime, DDoS protection, and the AI models used for chapter illustrations and premium narration.
  • Google — Gemini text and image generation when selected or used as a fallback.
  • DeepSeek and Anthropic — text generation, editing or quality review when configured by TubeBook.

Audiobook narration

If you narrate a book, the text of that book is sent to a text-to-speech provider so it can be spoken. Nothing else goes with it — no name, no email, no account identifier — and the provider returns audio and retains no rights in it. Which provider depends on the voice you chose, and on availability:

  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — the standard voices.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI (Deepgram Aura) — the premium voices, processed on the same infrastructure that already hosts the app.
  • OpenAI — used only as a fallback, when the provider for your chosen voice is unavailable. Under OpenAI's API terms this content is not used to train their models.

Narration is optional and only ever runs when you ask for it on a specific book. If you would rather your text never reach a speech provider, do not narrate that book.

Stock photographs and user artwork

If you choose Stock photos, TubeBook sends a descriptive search query derived from the book or chapter topic to one or more supported libraries. It does not send your name, email or account identifier. Search results may include the provider's image identifier, photographer name, source URL and attribution URL; TubeBook stores those details with the selected image so required credit and links can be included in the editor and exported book.

  • Unsplash — stock-photo search, download tracking required by its API guidelines, and linked photographer attribution.
  • Pexels and Pixabay — stock-photo search and source attribution where available.

If you upload your own artwork, the file is stored privately with the book and used only for the cover or chapter placement you choose. You are responsible for having the rights to use it.

Topic research

When you use Find a topic, the topic text you enter (and phrases derived from it) is sent to the services below in order to look up market data. Your name, email, account identifier and source material are not sent — these requests are made by our server and carry no information identifying you.

  • Amazon — public search-suggestion service, to see what shoppers type.
  • Google — public search-suggestion service, for the same purpose.
  • Google Books API — to count and describe already-published titles.
  • YouTube Data API (Google) — to find candidate source videos.
  • Serper — Google search results, to find candidate source articles.

Each of these providers may log the query it receives under its own privacy policy. Because the query is a topic rather than personal data, and is sent without account identifiers, we consider this low-risk — but if you would rather a topic never leave our systems, do not enter it into Find a topic.

Publishing-risk check

To flag possible intellectual-property risks, your book's title, subtitle, description, keywords, categories and chapter headings are sent to the same AI providers described above. The body text of your chapters is not.

We do not send anything to a trademark-register service. Where the check names a brand, TubeBook shows you a link to the public register; following it is your choice, and the request is made by your browser rather than by us.

04Data retention

  • Generated ebooks are retained for 30 days, then archived/deleted. Download anything you want to keep.
  • Audiobook narration — the lossless masters and everything built from them — is deleted on the same 30-day clock as the book it belongs to. Audio is not kept longer than the ebook.
  • Topic research results are cached for 14 days, after which the same niche is researched afresh. The stored record of your own past searches remains available in your account until you delete your account.
  • Account and billing records are retained while your account is active and as required by law (typically up to 7 years for invoices).
  • You may request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 7).

05Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session management, and minimal analytics cookies to understand product usage. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

06Security

We use TLS in transit, encryption at rest, scoped database access (row-level security), and least-privilege server credentials. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at support@tubebook.org.

07Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access your data
Correct inaccuracies
Export your data
Delete your account
Restrict processing
Object to processing

Email support@tubebook.org to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days.

08International transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

09Children

TubeBook is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete the associated account promptly.

10Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.
