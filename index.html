Fidelity Modes

How closely should your ebook follow the video?

Three modes. Three legal postures. You choose how original the output needs to be.

Repurposing Only
High Fidelity

Your video, word-for-word structure

The ebook follows the exact structure, names, stories, and ideas of your transcript — ideal for repurposing your own content into a book.

Repurpose your own YouTube channel
Course material → companion guide
Lecture series → printed textbook
⚠ You must own the rights to the source video. Distributing High Fidelity output from third-party content without permission is copyright infringement.
Check Your Rights
Smart Adapt (default)

Same ideas, rebuilt structure

The AI rewrites and restructures the source — names may change, flow is rebuilt, but the core content stays close to the original.

Refresh older content for a new brand
Adapt a tutorial into a how-to guide
Repackage interviews into case studies
Commercial Ready
Low Fidelity

Inspired by the video, truly original

The video is a thematic seed only. The AI generates substantially original content — names, structure, and wording are all new. Safe for KDP, Gumroad, and commercial publishing.
