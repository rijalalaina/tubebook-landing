01The Service

TubeBook transforms source material you supply — YouTube videos, web page URLs, and transcripts or text you paste — into ebooks using AI. A single ebook may be generated from one source or from several combined into one project. The Service also offers optional topic research, which reports publicly available search-suggestion and published-book data for a niche you enter and suggests sources you may choose to use. Features, pricing, and credit costs may evolve over time.

05Topic research data & no commercial guarantee

What the data is. Topic research reports (a) phrases returned by the public search-suggestion services of Amazon and Google, and (b) counts and details of already-published titles from the Google Books API. These are indicators of search interest. They are not sales figures, sales ranks, revenue estimates, or royalty projections.

Why not Amazon Best Seller Rank. Amazon does not make Best Seller Rank data publicly available to services like ours: the Product Advertising API was retired in May 2026 and its replacement requires an Amazon Associates account with ongoing qualifying sales in the account holder's own name. We therefore do not provide, and do not claim to provide, Amazon sales rank. Any figure labelled a score in the Service is a ranking aid for ordering your own shortlist.

No guarantee of results. Nothing in topic research is a prediction, promise or guarantee of sales, revenue, rankings, visibility or commercial success of any book you publish. Publishing outcomes depend on factors entirely outside our control, including your writing, cover, pricing, marketing, timing, marketplace algorithms and competition. You are solely responsible for your commercial decisions.

Accuracy and availability. The underlying data comes from third parties, is not under our control, may be incomplete, out of date or unavailable, and may change or cease without notice. Suggested sources are supplied for convenience only — we do not endorse them, warrant their accuracy, and you remain responsible under section 06 for holding the necessary rights in every source you choose to use.
