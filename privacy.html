This Privacy Policy explains how TubeBook ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and applications (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

Section 1

Information we collect

Account data

  • Email address, display name, and password hash (or OAuth identifier from Google/GitHub sign-in).
  • Billing data handled by Stripe — we never store or see your full card numbers.

Content you provide

  • YouTube URLs and transcripts you submit for processing.
  • Generated ebooks, covers, chapter images, and related metadata.

Technical data

  • IP address, browser and device information, log timestamps.
  • Usage events such as generations started, credits consumed, and errors encountered.
Section 2

How we use information

  • To operate the Service: process generations, deliver exports, track your credit balance.
  • To bill subscriptions and credit packs via Stripe.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, billing receipts, security alerts).
  • To debug issues, secure the platform, and improve generation quality.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your content for advertising purposes.

Section 3

AI processing & sub-processors

To generate ebooks, content you submit (YouTube URL or transcript) is sent to AI model providers through our AI gateway. These providers process the content solely to return generated results and do not use it to train their foundation models under our enterprise terms.

Section 4

Data retention

  • Generated ebooks are retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted. Please download anything you want to keep.
  • Account and billing records are retained while your account is active and as required by law (typically up to 7 years for invoices).
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 7.
Section 5

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session management, and minimal analytics cookies to understand how the product is used. We do not run third-party advertising trackers or retargeting pixels.

Section 6

Security

We protect your data using TLS in transit, encryption at rest, row-level security in our database, and least-privilege server credentials. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at support@tubebook.org.

Section 7

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and equivalent laws), you have rights to access, correct, export, restrict processing of, or delete your personal data.

✉️ Email support@tubebook.org to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days.
Section 8

International transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and the European Union. Where required by law, such transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent approved safeguards.

Section 9

Children

TubeBook is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware of such collection we will delete it promptly.

Section 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
