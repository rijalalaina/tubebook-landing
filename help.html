Help & Tutorials

Everything you need to turn YouTube videos into professional ebooks in just a few minutes.

Quick start

1
Create a free account at app.tubebook.org. No credit card required.
2
Paste a YouTube URL on the Generate page, choose a format (Lead Magnet or Commercial) and a cover style.
3
Download your ebook in PDF, DOCX, EPUB and PPTX within minutes.

Lead Magnet vs Commercial

Lead Magnet ~5,000 words

Short format, perfect to grow your email list. Fast generation, condensed structure, ideal as a signup gift.

Commercial 25,000+ words

Long format, ready to sell. Detailed chapters, illustrations, editorial consistency — built for Amazon KDP, Gumroad or your own site.

Cover style

Before generating, pick a cover preset — palette, typography, and layout. The chosen style is applied consistently across all exported formats.

Side-by-side preview

From your dashboard, click Preview to open a comparison view of all 4 formats (PDF, DOCX, EPUB, PPTX) before downloading. Handy for checking layout without downloading anything first.

Privacy & file retention

24-hour download links

Signed download URLs expire after 24 hours. After that, the app automatically refreshes them on demand.

30-day storage

Your files are automatically deleted from storage after 30 days. A countdown is shown on each ebook card, turning orange in the last 3 days.

Permanent delete with ZIP export

You can delete your ebooks at any time. Before permanent deletion, TubeBook offers a ZIP archive containing all formats plus the cover image.

FAQ

How long does generation take?

Lead Magnet: 3–8 minutes. Commercial ebook: 15–30 minutes depending on source video length.

Which formats can I export?

PDF, DOCX (Word), EPUB (e-readers / Kindle via conversion), and PPTX (slides). The cover is also downloadable separately in high resolution.

What are the plan quotas?
  • Free: 1 lead magnet lifetime, TubeBook watermark
  • Starter ($9/mo): 10 lead magnets/month, no watermark
  • Pro ($29/mo): 5 commercial ebooks + 5 lead magnets/month, all formats, priority generation
Can I generate several ebooks in parallel?

Free: 1 at a time. Starter: 2 in parallel. Pro: 4 in parallel. Extra generations are queued and start automatically when a slot opens.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Authentication is required for all access. File access is protected by row-level security (each user only sees their own ebooks). Data is encrypted at rest, and files are automatically deleted after 30 days.

Can I sell the generated ebooks?

Yes, with a paid plan (Starter or Pro) which removes the watermark. The ebooks you generate are yours to keep and sell — provided the source content rights allow it. See our Terms for full details on fidelity modes and copyright responsibility.
