How the numbers are worked out

Spine width = page count × paper thickness: 0.002252" per page on white paper, 0.0025" on cream.

Full-wrap cover width = 0.125" bleed + back cover + spine + front cover + 0.125" bleed. Height = trim height + 0.25" (0.125" bleed top and bottom). At 300 DPI, multiply inches by 300 for the pixel size.

Page count from words uses typical book typography for each trim size at a 10 pt calibration (about 340 words per page on 6" × 9"), scaled for larger body text. Front matter, chapter openings and images add pages, so treat it as an estimate.

KDP limits: a paperback needs at least 24 pages, and KDP allows text on the spine only when the book has more than 79 pages. A large-print book needs body text of at least 16 pt. Always confirm the final figure with KDP's own cover template before uploading.

The template marks the 0.125" bleed (background art must reach the outer edge), the trim line, a safe area 0.125" inside the trim for text and important images, 0.0625" of clearance on each side of the spine text, and the 2" × 1.2" area at the bottom right of the back cover where KDP prints the barcode.
