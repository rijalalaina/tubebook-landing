The Process

From video to ebook
in a single pipeline

A fully automated chain of AI steps handles every stage — you just review and download.

Drop in any YouTube video link, add your author name, and choose lead magnet or commercial ebook mode.

02

AI Writes & Structures

Our AI arsenal transcribes the video, generates a chapter outline, then writes up to 25,000 words of polished content.
